## The Idea

Transform a standard cargo van into a fully functional mobile living space equipped with modern amenities and smart home technology. The goal is to create a comfortable, efficient, and sustainable living environment on wheels.

## The Inspiration

The desire for freedom and flexibility in lifestyle, combined with advances in compact technology and renewable energy, made this project both practical and exciting. The pandemic accelerated remote work trends, making van life a viable option for many professionals.

## Features

- **Solar Power System**: 400W solar panels with lithium battery bank for off-grid capability
- **Smart Climate Control**: Automated heating and cooling based on temperature sensors
- **Water Management**: Fresh water tank with filtration system and grey water monitoring
- **Space Optimization**: Custom cabinetry and convertible furniture for maximum utility
- **Connectivity**: Starlink internet and cellular boosters for reliable remote work
- **Security**: GPS tracking and smart locks with remote monitoring

## Technical Details

The electrical system is the heart of the conversion, featuring:
- Victron Energy charge controller and inverter system
- LiFePO4 battery bank (400Ah capacity)
- Shore power integration for campground hookups
- DC-DC charging from alternator while driving

Custom automation built with Home Assistant monitors:
- Battery state of charge
- Water tank levels
- Interior temperature and humidity
- Security sensors

## Next Steps

Future enhancements planned:
- Add hydronic heating system for winter camping
- Install roof vent fan with rain sensor
- Implement grey water recycling system
- Upgrade to higher capacity battery bank
